About the project

Built to help learners
find their path

SkillPilot AI is an intelligent career guidance system that uses machine learning to analyze your skill profile and generate personalized learning roadmaps — removing the guesswork from your career development.

Technology Stack

At its core, SkillPilot uses TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity to match your skills against role-specific requirements. This gives us a nuanced, weight-aware score rather than a simple keyword count.

TF-IDF Cosine Similarity Python Flask Chart.js

Purpose

Most learners spend weeks figuring out what to learn before they even start learning. SkillPilot collapses that uncertainty into a clear, structured roadmap — personalized to exactly where you are right now.

What You Get

  • Skill gap score with visual breakdown
  • AI-generated insights and recommendations
  • Prioritized learning path by role
  • Month-by-month timeline planning
  • Curated free & paid course links
